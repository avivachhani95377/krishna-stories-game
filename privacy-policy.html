Privacy Policy · Effective 15 November 2026

At a glance
  • No account or sign-up. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or location.
  • Your game progress, coins and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The free game shows ads from Google AdMob and uses Google Firebase to count app usage and to adjust ad settings. These Google services use device identifiers such as the advertising ID.
  • We do not sell personal information.

This policy explains how the Krishna Stories app for Android (package com.krishnastories.games), "the app", handles information. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean the developer of Krishna Stories. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do not collect

  • We have no user accounts and do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or date of birth.
  • The app does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, files or precise (GPS) location.
  • The app has no chat and no way for players to share personal information with each other.

2. Information stored on your device

Your story progress, coins, hints, sound and language settings are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. We do not receive this data.

  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.
  • If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this data in your own device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your account settings, not by us.

4. Ads

  • The game is free and shows ads: a banner, full-screen ads between levels, and optional "watch an ad" rewards such as a hint, a second chance or extra coins.
  • Rewarded ads are always the player's choice.
  • All ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences (G).
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads or related data use.
  • You can limit personalised ads anywhere:
    • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
    • Or turn off ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com.

5. Contacting support

If you use Settings → Support / Feedback, the app opens your email app with a message addressed to us. If you send it, we receive:

  • your email address and your message;
  • the app version;
  • your device model and Android version;
  • the number of stories and levels you have completed.

We use this only to answer you and fix problems, and we delete it once it is no longer needed.

6. Children

  • Krishna Stories is a family-friendly game.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app never asks players for personal details.
  • Links that leave the app (rating, support, this policy) are behind a simple grown-up check.
  • If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example in a support email, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information is shared only:

  • with the Google services described above, so the app can work and show ads;
  • when required by law.

8. Data retention and security

  • Data on your device stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Google keeps analytics and advertising data according to its own retention settings and policies.
  • Information sent by the app to Google services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

9. Your choices and rights

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads at any time (see section 4).
  • You can delete all game data by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access or delete personal information. Contact us and we will help, including with requests to Google about data linked to the app.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
